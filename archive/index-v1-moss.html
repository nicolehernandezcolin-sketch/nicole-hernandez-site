About

Afro-Cuban
R&B

between two cities

Nicole Hernandez is a singer-songwriter who grew up between Tijuana and San Diego, the daughter of a Cuban father and a mother from Sinaloa, Mexico. The salsa, reggaeton and hip-hop that filled her home now live in her sound: Afro-Cuban rhythm woven through Latin jazz, R&B, electropop and soul.

Her debut album Layers is a dreamy mix of poetry and orchestration, written in both English and Spanish, about identity, autonomy and becoming. She performs it with an all-women band she brought together from musicians across San Diego.

Nicole was highlighted by KPBS as one of the standout San Diego artists in the NPR Tiny Desk Contest, performed at the KPBS San Diego Book Festival, and is the founder of NEXUS, a San Diego community celebrating music, dance and film.
