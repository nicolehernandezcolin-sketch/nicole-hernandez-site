About

Meet Nicole

Nicole Hernandez is a singer-songwriter and creative entrepreneur based in San Diego, California. She grew up between Tijuana and San Diego, the daughter of a Cuban father and a mother from Sinaloa, Mexico. The salsa, reggaeton and hip-hop that filled her home now live in her music: Afro-Cuban rhythm woven through R&B, Latin jazz, electropop and soul.

Her debut album Layers (2025) is a dreamy mix of poetry and orchestration about identity, autonomy and becoming, written in English and Spanish. She performs it with an all-women band of musicians from across San Diego, and brings each song to life through her own music videos.

KPBS named her one of the standout San Diego artists in the NPR Tiny Desk Contest and featured her on The Finest. Off stage, she is the founder and director of NEXUS, a community platform for music, dance, film, fashion and sport.
